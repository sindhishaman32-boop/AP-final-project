Pakistan is blessed with more than 1,050 kilometers of diverse coastline along the Arabian Sea—stretching from the mangrove estuaries and turtle sanctuaries of Sindh to the dramatic geological cliffs and pristine turquoise waters of Balochistan.

BeautyOfBeaches was engineered as an all-in-one digital tourism ecosystem. We provide real-time marine weather, transparent travel pricing in Pakistani Rupees (PKR), integrated local payment options (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Raast), and verified travel safety protocols.

Our Journey

Historic Growth Timeline

How BeautyOfBeaches evolved into Pakistan's foremost coastal travel authority.

2022
Genesis & Makran Highway Reconnaissance

Conducted foundational field mapping of 800+ kilometers of the Makran Coastal Highway. Documented the natural topography of Kund Malir, Ormara, and Pasni.

2023
Marine Safety & Ecotourism Certification

Formally partnered with local coastal rescue organizations, trained certified marine guides, and instituted our Clean Coast Pakistan conservation charter.

2024
Launch of Hawkesbay & Gwadar Private Chalets

Acquired exclusive operating rights for premium oceanfront chalets at Hawkesbay Beach Karachi and introduced luxury glamping camps at Kund Malir.

2025
Global Tropical Destination Expansion

Integrated five-star overwater resort partnerships in the Maldives, Bora Bora, and the Mediterranean, allowing Pakistani travelers to book international paradises in PKR.

2026
Unified Digital Gateway & Apple-Grade UI

Launched our Apple-inspired frosted glass platform, with instant JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Raast checkouts and 24/7 dedicated coastal concierge care.

Our Clean Coast Pakistan Initiative

We pledge to preserve the fragile marine biodiversity of the Arabian Sea. For every booking made on BeautyOfBeaches, we sponsor local coastal cleanup drives across Hawkesbay, Kund Malir, and Gwadar.
