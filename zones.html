Explore Coastal Zones

From the dramatic 1,050 km Arabian Sea shoreline of Pakistan (West Zone) to the celebrated tropical archipelagos of Southeast Asia, Polynesia, and the Indian Ocean.

West Zone

Arabian Sea & Americas

Home to Pakistan’s dramatic 1,050 km coastline: Kund Malir, Gwadar Hammerhead, Hawkesbay Beach, Astola Island, plus Rio de Janeiro.

Best Season: Oct – Mar
Water Temp: 25°C – 27°C
From Karachi: Direct Highway
North Zone

Tropical Karsts & Europe

Towering limestone formations and pristine coves in Thailand (Phi Phi, Railay), Greece (Navagio), and Zanzibar (Nungwi).

Best Season: Nov – Apr
Water Temp: 24°C – 29°C
Flight from PK: 4 – 7 Hours
East Zone

Lagoon Luxury & Coral

The world’s most celebrated overwater luxury lagoons: Maldives private coral atolls, Bora Bora turquoise waters, and Arugam Bay surfing.

Best Season: Dec – May
Water Temp: 28°C – 29°C
Coastal Vibe: Ultra-Luxury & Surf
South Zone

Silica Sands & Whales

Purest silica sands in Australia (Whitehaven), rainforest-lined Andaman shores (Radhanagar), and whale watching in Sri Lanka (Mirissa).

Best Season: Sep – Apr
Water Temp: 26°C – 28°C
Highlights: Marine Reserves
