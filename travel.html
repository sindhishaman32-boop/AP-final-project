Beach Travel Advice

Essential Safety & Preparation for Pakistan Coast

The Balochistan and Sindh coastlines offer some of the most untamed natural wonders in South Asia. Follow these official tips for a safe and memorable journey:

Original CNIC / Passport

Always carry original identity cards for security checkpoints on the Makran Coastal Highway (N-10).

Fuel & Cash Supplies

Refuel in Hub or Winder before heading towards Kund Malir; ATMs are sparse beyond Ormara.

Arabian Sea Tide Awareness

Check the tide schedule before swimming. Avoid swimming during monsoon season (June – August).
